Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Purpose

Kanon Cloud Sync is a personal application used by its owner to synchronize and back up files between Google Drive and Microsoft OneDrive.

Google User Data

The application may access Google Drive files and related file metadata as necessary to perform synchronization.

Use of Data

Google user data is used solely to provide file synchronization and backup functionality requested by the owner of the application.

Storage and Transfer

Files accessed from Google Drive may be copied to Microsoft OneDrive as part of the synchronization process. Local synchronization state and logs may also be stored on the owner's personal computer.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties. Data is transferred only as necessary between the owner's Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, and personal computer to provide synchronization functionality.

Data Retention

Data remains under the control of the owner and may be deleted from the connected cloud services or personal computer by the owner.

Access

This application is intended solely for personal use by its owner.
